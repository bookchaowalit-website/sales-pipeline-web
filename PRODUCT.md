# Sales Pipeline — Product Truth

Sales Pipeline is a local deal desk for reviewing sample opportunities, their current stage, next action, and expected value. It is a workflow interface demo, not a connected CRM or revenue forecast.

## User and job

- Audience: a solo operator reviewing a small set of active prospects.
- Primary job: select a deal, understand what is blocking it, and record the next action without losing context.
- Mode: operate.

## Boundaries

- Deal names, values, and contacts are synthetic.
- No email sync, team permissions, payment data, or probabilistic forecast is claimed.
- The first release is client-side and uses an explicit sample dataset.
